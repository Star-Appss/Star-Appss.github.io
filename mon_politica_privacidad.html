Privacy Policy of the app
I Miss You Mom - Mourning

Last updated: August 2026

1. INTRODUCTION

Welcome to I Miss You Mom. This application is designed to offer a space of remembrance and comfort, with quotes, images, videos and poems to honor the memory of those who are no longer with us. By using our application, you accept the terms and conditions described in this Privacy Policy.

2. INFORMATION COLLECTION

2.1 Information we collect:

  • Technical record of the acceptance status of these policies on the device to manage initial access.
  • Application content (quotes, images, videos and poems) downloaded from our servers in JSON format and temporarily stored in the device cache for display, without such information being associated with any user.
  • Technical advertising data managed by the Google AdMob SDK (see section 6).

2.2 Information we do NOT collect:

  • Any kind of personally identifiable information, such as names, email addresses, accounts or phone numbers.
  • Geographic location data or device movement tracking.
  • Contact list, personal media files, web browsing history or information from other installed applications.

3. USE OF INFORMATION

The information collected is used exclusively to:

  • Personalize the reading and viewing experience in real time according to the font size and visual theme selected by the user.
  • Enable the proper functioning of searches, filters, favorites, highlights and associated notes.
  • Ensure the persistence and automatic synchronization of user settings across the different screens of the application.

4. DATA STORAGE

To guarantee your privacy, the application works locally and does not require a permanent Internet connection to handle your information. Data is stored strictly locally on your device through:

  • DataStore (local preferences): Used solely to remember whether the user has accepted the privacy policies.
  • Device cache: The application content (quotes, images, videos and poems) downloaded from our servers in JSON format is temporarily stored in the device cache for display, without being associated with any user.

None of your data is transferred, shared or uploaded to external servers under any circumstances, except for the initial content download from our servers and the technical communication of the advertising SDK described in section 6.

5. PERMISSIONS

The application requests the following specific permissions, declared in its manifest:

  • Internet / Network access (INTERNET): Required only to download the content in JSON format from our servers, load images and videos, and display advertising. Once the content is downloaded, it is accessible without an internet connection.
  • Network state access (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required by the advertising SDK to determine connectivity and display appropriate ads.

The application does not request storage, location, camera, microphone or contacts permissions. Downloading or sharing videos is done through a temporary file provider (FileProvider) without requiring additional permissions.

6. ADVERTISING

Our application displays ads provided by Google AdMob in order to support the free development and technical maintenance of the application for the user. By using the application, you accept the inclusion of these ads.

  • The Google AdMob SDK and its advertising partners may collect and process device advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), IP address, device type, ad interaction data and other technical information in order to display personalized or non-personalized ads, according to the applicable mobile platform regulations and your device settings.
  • Ads are presented discreetly (banners at the bottom of the main screens and occasional interstitials with controlled frequency) and do not interrupt essential content or system navigation.
  • You can learn more about how Google handles data in the Google Privacy Policy and about ads in the Google Ads Policies.
  • You can limit ad personalization from your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID / Opt out of personalized ads).

7. SECURITY

The information that the application handles locally (the policy acceptance status and the cached content) does not leave your phone, which drastically reduces the risk of external leaks. However, the security of such data also depends on the care and access protection you maintain over your own device. The information processed by the advertising SDK is governed by the privacy policies of Google and its partners.

8. PRIVACY OF MINORS

This application provides emotional and reflective content suitable for the whole family. We do not intentionally collect any kind of personally identifiable information from minors or any other user.

9. CHANGES TO THE POLICY

We reserve the right to modify this policy at any time to adapt it to future technical updates of the application, including changes in advertising providers. Any substantial change will take effect immediately upon publishing the new version of this text within the application.
